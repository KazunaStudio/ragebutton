RAGE BUTTON

Windows:
1. Extract this folder somewhere permanent.
2. Double-click "Rage Button.bat".
3. Your default browser will open the Rage Button.
4. Press it whenever necessary.

Your button name, click count, timestamps, and history are saved in your
browser's local storage. They remain after closing the browser.

Important:
- If you clear browser/site data, the saved rage history may be removed.
- Keep opening index.html in the same browser for the same saved history.
